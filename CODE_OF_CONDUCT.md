# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want participation in this project to be a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainer through the contact method listed on the repository's GitHub page. Reports will be reviewed and handled as respectfully and confidentially as possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct and may block contributors for behavior that is inappropriate or harmful.
